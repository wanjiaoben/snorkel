Snorkeling gear and underwater photos.

Cancellation
Safety first: the captain decides whether we sail. If the sea or weather makes the tour impossible (typhoon etc.), you can reschedule or receive a full refund. Cancellation by the guest: 14+ days before — full refund; 13–10 days — 50% fee; 9–6 days — 80% fee; within 5 days — no refund. Personal illness (e.g. a cold or upset stomach) is not covered by the weather policy. Visa refusal: full refund.

FAQ

Q: Where do we meet?

A: Ginowan Marina (宜野湾マリーナ), the same marina as the Kerama trip.

Q: How long is the boat ride?

A: Less than 20 minutes, in nearshore waters.

Q: What time is the charter?

A: Around 17:00–19:00, depending on the sunset time that day. The charter is about 2 hours.

Q: Is this private or shared?

A: Private charter only. This is not a shared boat.

Q: How much is it?

A: From ¥60,000 tax included. Please ask for a quote.

Q: How many guests can join?

A: Up to 12 guests.

Q: What is included?

A: Snorkeling gear and underwater photos are included. Drinks are not included.

Q: Is drone photography included?

A: No. Drone flights are prohibited in the nearshore waters used for this charter, so this charter does not include drone photography.

Q: Can children join?

A: Yes. Children can join.

Q: What is the cancellation policy?

A: Safety first: the captain decides whether we sail. If the sea or weather makes the tour impossible (typhoon etc.), you can reschedule or receive a full refund. Cancellation by the guest: 14+ days before — full refund; 13–10 days — 50% fee; 9–6 days — 80% fee; within 5 days — no refund. Personal illness (e.g. a cold or upset stomach) is not covered by the weather policy. Visa refusal: full refund.
